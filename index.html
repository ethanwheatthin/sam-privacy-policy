Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 16, 2024

This Privacy Policy describes how S.A.M (Sports Ad Muter) ("we", "our", or "the extension") handles your information when you use our Chrome extension.

Privacy First: S.A.M is designed with your privacy as a top priority. All processing happens locally on your machine, and no data is sent to external servers.

1. Information Collection and Use

1.1 What We Collect

S.A.M does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The extension operates entirely on your local machine and does not have access to:

  • Your browsing history
  • Personal identification information
  • Login credentials
  • Payment information
  • Any data from other websites or services

1.2 Video Analysis Process

The extension performs the following operations locally:

  • Video Detection: Identifies the active video element on the page you're viewing
  • Screenshot Capture: Takes periodic screenshots of the video using HTML5 canvas technology
  • Local AI Analysis: Sends screenshots to your locally-running Ollama AI instance (qwen3-vl:2b model) for analysis
  • Audio Control: Mutes or unmutes the video based on whether gameplay or advertisements are detected

1.3 Temporary Data

Screenshots captured for analysis are:

  • Processed in real-time
  • Sent only to your local Ollama instance (default: http://localhost:11434)
  • Never stored permanently
  • Never transmitted to external servers
  • Immediately discarded after analysis

2. How Information is Used

The extension uses video screenshots exclusively for:

  • Determining whether active football gameplay or commercials are currently displayed
  • Automatically controlling video audio (mute/unmute) based on content detection

All analysis occurs locally on your machine through your Ollama installation. The extension does not use this data for any other purpose.

3. Data Storage and Retention

3.1 Local Storage

The extension stores the following settings locally in your browser:

  • Ollama API URL configuration (default: http://localhost:11434)
  • Check interval settings (how frequently video is analyzed)
  • Extension state (monitoring active/inactive)

These settings are stored using Chrome's local storage API and remain on your device. They are not synced or transmitted anywhere.

3.2 No Long-term Data Retention

S.A.M does not retain any video screenshots, analysis results, or viewing history beyond the immediate processing moment.

4. Third-Party Services

4.1 Ollama Integration

S.A.M requires a local installation of Ollama with the qwen3-vl:2b model. This integration:

  • Runs entirely on your local machine
  • Does not involve any cloud services
  • Communicates only with your localhost (default port: 11434)
  • Can be configured to use a custom local URL if needed

For information about Ollama's privacy practices, please visit: https://ollama.ai

4.2 No External Data Sharing

S.A.M does not:

  • Share data with third parties
  • Use analytics or tracking services
  • Connect to remote servers (except your local Ollama instance)
  • Transmit information outside your device

5. Permissions

The extension requires certain Chrome permissions to function:

  • activeTab: To detect and interact with video elements on the current page
  • scripting: To inject content scripts that monitor video playback
  • storage: To save your settings locally

These permissions are used exclusively for the extension's core functionality and not for data collection.

6. Security

We take security seriously:

  • All processing occurs locally on your machine
  • No data is transmitted over the internet
  • Communication with Ollama uses localhost connections
  • No external servers or databases are involved
  • The extension does not create any network vulnerabilities

7. Children's Privacy

S.A.M does not knowingly collect information from children or anyone else, as it does not collect personal information at all. The extension is suitable for all ages.

8. Your Rights and Choices

You have complete control over the extension:

  • Start/Stop: Enable or disable monitoring at any time
  • Configure: Adjust check intervals and Ollama URL settings
  • Uninstall: Remove the extension completely from Chrome at any time
  • Data Deletion: Uninstalling the extension removes all locally stored settings

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

  • Open an issue on our GitHub repository: https://github.com/ethanwheatthin/Football-Ad-Muter
